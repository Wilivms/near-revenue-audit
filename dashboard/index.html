For every 100 NEAR issued, NEAR Intents revenue buys back ….

NEAR's protocol revenue is real: it reconciles to the chain within 0.1%. It is also growing, mostly because NEAR now charges more on the flows it controls. But at today's market cap it offsets only a fraction of the 2.4% annual issuance. This page checks that claim against live data every time it loads.

What would prove this thesis wrong

Three kill criteria were written before the analysis (HYPOTHESES.md). Each is invalidating if it holds for 30 consecutive days. Two are checked live; one needs swap-level data.

Live on every visit: DefiLlama (fees, revenue, holders revenue and volume of NEAR Intents), DefiLlama coins (NEAR price), NEAR RPC (total supply). From the repository snapshot : monthly issuance (archival RPC), 1Click swap data (NEAR Intents Explorer API), Monte Carlo outputs.

  • 1Click covers about 28% of Intents volume and no confidential swaps.
  • "Bought back" counts NEAR entering the 3 revenue wallets. Only one of them is a buyback wallet; the front-end fund (about 47% of all-time NEAR captured) is a DAO treasury with no on-chain commitment not to sell.
  • Live "bought back" converts dollars at today's NEAR price.
