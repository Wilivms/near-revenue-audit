04 / Pricing

NEAR's cut doubled in September

Captured revenue in basis points of volume. Volume did not drive the rise. Mostly, confidential swaps did: they went from 13% to 41% of volume between August and October and pay NEAR far more than public swaps. On 1Click, NEAR also repriced (a 20 bps Trust Wallet ZEC flow, higher fees on swaps without an integrator).

Read with care

  • 1Click covers about 28% of Intents volume and no confidential swaps.
  • "Bought back" counts NEAR entering the 3 revenue wallets. Only one is a buyback wallet; the front-end fund (about 47% of all-time NEAR captured) is a DAO treasury with no on-chain commitment not to sell.
  • The live reading converts dollars at today's NEAR price.
